How It Works

Up and running in under 5 minutes.

No complex setup. No account required to get started. Open the app and your library is ready.

1

Download & Open MedShelf

Install from the App Store or Play Store. On first launch, a quick tutorial shows you the main features — takes about 30 seconds. You pick your specialty focus (MBBS, MD, Nursing etc.) so MedShelf shows the most relevant topics first.

Takes 30 seconds
2

Import or Scan Your Files

Three ways to get content in:

Find on Device — MedShelf scans WhatsApp, Telegram, Downloads & 8 more folders, surfaces every medical file already on your phone, and lets you bulk-import.

Scan — Tap the Scan button to snap a paper document with the camera. Edge detection, perspective correction and contrast enhancement happen on-device via Google ML Kit. Multi-page captures auto-stitch into a single PDF.

Share — While reading anywhere on your phone, hit Share → MedShelf and the file lands inside the right specialty.

Three ways in. Zero friction.
3

Organise by Specialty & Topic

Files land in the correct specialty folder automatically. Tap Browse by Specialty or Browse by Topic on the home screen to navigate your library. Each specialty has sub-topics — e.g. Cardiology → ECG, Heart Failure, Valvular Disease.

Structure mirrors your curriculum
4

Capture Rich-Text Notes

Tap the Quick Note box on the home screen. The editor gives you bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, bullet/numbered lists, headers, blockquotes, indents, undo/redo, and 8 highlight tints. Pick a topic, save — your formatted note lives next to your PDFs in that specialty.

Notes that actually look like notes
5

Study, then Share with Colleagues

Open any specialty, see all your files and notes together with thumbnail previews. Tap a PDF to read it, tap a note to edit it. Every file row has a visible Share button — send to WhatsApp, Email, Telegram, Drive, or any app on your phone in two taps.

Your library, your way
